Our history

Cantapueblo was born in the city of Godoy Cruz, Mendoza, in 1989.

1989

First edition in Godoy Cruz, with 15 choirs from Mendoza and one guest choir from Chile.

1990

Gloria Brastchi and Alejandro Scarpetta write "El Canto Grande," the festival's official anthem, performed by thousands of voices over more than 30 years.

1993

Fundación COPPLA takes over the organization, and the gathering expands across the whole province of Mendoza with over 100 choirs a year.

1997

First edition held outside Mendoza: Viña del Mar, Chile, hosts the gathering.

1998 and 2001

Quito, Ecuador, hosts Cantapueblo on two occasions.

Our first chords

2000

UNESCO names Cantapueblo a "Landmark Event for the Culture of Peace," as part of its Action Programme for a Culture of Peace.

2008

A double international edition: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, and Guarenas, Venezuela.

2018

Panama City hosts that year's edition.

Today

More than 35 years of history, with choirs from Argentina, Chile, Brazil, Cuba, Uruguay, Ecuador, Panama, Colombia, Venezuela, Mexico, Slovenia, Sweden, Belgium, Italy, Spain, the United States, Malaysia, Germany, the Netherlands, the Czech Republic and other countries.
