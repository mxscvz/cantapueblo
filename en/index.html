An international choral festival

Cantapueblo is an international choral gathering that promotes friendship, cooperation and understanding between peoples through choral singing. Every edition brings together children's, youth, adult, professional and amateur choirs, in theaters, churches, cultural centers and open-air venues across Mendoza.

What we're after

Widening our horizon. We're the choral festival most chosen by our Latin American peers — four continents are still left to explore.

What we do

We work as a coordinated team, and it shows in every detail of the festival. We cultivate the festive spirit that has defined Cantapueblo since its origins. We build lasting relationships: a large share of participating choirs choose to return in later editions.

What sets us apart

Leading choral activity in Argentina. Pioneers in Latin America and the world. More than 35 years of history. A quality service, always.

El Canto Grande

In 1990, Gloria Brastchi and Alejandro Scarpetta wrote Cantapueblo's official song, performed by thousands of voices over more than 30 years.

Singing at our festival is an experience you will never forget. The emotion runs high in every concert. Your choir, the beautiful landscapes, good wine and the warmth of our people are the perfect alchemy to celebrate life at Cantapueblo.

Alejandro Scarpetta — Founder and Artistic Director
