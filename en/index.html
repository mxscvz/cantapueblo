Cantapueblo, the Choral Feast of America

A gathering that sings friendship between peoples.

35+ years bringing together choirs from around the world in Mendoza, Argentina.

A festival, not a competition

Cantapueblo is a non-competitive international choral gathering that promotes friendship, cooperation and understanding between peoples through choral singing. Every edition brings together children's, youth, adult, professional and amateur choirs, performing in theaters, plazas and cultural venues across Mendoza.

Recognized in the choral world

Regional leadership

A benchmark for choral activity in Argentina, and one of Latin America's most-attended choral festivals.

UNESCO recognition

Named a "Landmark Event for the Culture of Peace" by UNESCO in the year 2000.

International reach

Choirs from more than twenty countries have taken part across the festival's editions.
