What a week at Cantapueblo looks like

The festival's typical structure, with concerts in different formats and venues — from the city to the mountains. Exact dates and venues for the 2027 edition will be confirmed later.

Day 1

Welcome concert

Choirs arriving a day early take the opening slot; everyone else attends as a special guest.

Day 2

Concert of the Peoples

Each choir shares the stage with others in a joint performance.

Day 3

Concert in the Heights

At the foot of Cerro Aconcagua, in direct contact with the mountain — one of the most memorable moments for visiting choirs.

Day 3 (evening)

Concert for Peace

An evening performance shared among several of the festival's choirs.

Day 4

Street Cantata

Choirs perform as soloists across different open-air spaces in downtown Mendoza.

Closing

Final concert

Each choir performs its own piece, then joins the Grand Choir of Cantapueblo, singing "El Canto Grande" together with everyone else.
