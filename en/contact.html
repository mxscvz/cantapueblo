Frequently asked questions

How can my choir take part?

Write to us via WhatsApp, email or Instagram with information about your choir. Our team will send you the details of the next edition and the steps to take part.

When does the festival take place?

Cantapueblo is held every year at the beginning of November, in the province of Mendoza, Argentina.

Is there a participation cost?

Foreign choirs pay a participation fee that covers the coordination of their stay in Mendoza, participation in the concerts, and tourist activities. Details are arranged directly with the organization.

How do I get to Mendoza?

Mendoza has an international airport (El Plumerillo Airport), with domestic and international flight connections.
