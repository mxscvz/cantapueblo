Frequently asked questions

How can my choir take part?

Write to us by email or Instagram and tell us about your choir. We'll send you all the information about the next edition.

When does the festival take place?

Cantapueblo is held every November, in Mendoza, Argentina.

Is there a participation cost?

Foreign choirs pay a participation fee that covers the concert and the coordination of their stay in Mendoza. Details are arranged directly with the organization.

How do I get to Mendoza?

Mendoza has an international airport (El Plumerillo Airport), with domestic and international connections.
