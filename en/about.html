About us

Cantapueblo is organized by a small team, with more than 35 years of work behind it. These are the people leading the festival.

Alejandro Scarpetta

Artistic Director

Co-founder of the Festival alongside León Repetur. Choir conductor, composer and choral arranger. He was artistic director of the Cine Teatro Plaza, the Centro Cultural Luis Sandrini, the Teatro Independencia and the Teatro La Compañía.

León Repetur

Director of Institutional Relations

Cultural manager, winemaker and pharmacist. Co-founded the Festival alongside Alejandro Scarpetta. He was Secretary of Culture for the Province of Mendoza.

Gustavo Aguilera

Executive Producer

Senior Technician in Sociocultural Management and educator. He has been Cantapueblo's Executive Producer since 2006, and General Coordinator of Cantapueblo Niños since its first edition. He is the founding president of the Gestores Culturales de Mendoza association.
