About us

Cantapueblo exists thanks to the dedication of those who have led it since its first edition. A team of professionals convinced that choral music is a vehicle for social and cultural transformation.

Alejandro Scarpetta

Artistic Director

Co-founder of the Cantapueblo Festival. Choir conductor, composer and choral arranger. He was artistic director of the Cine Teatro Plaza, the Centro Cultural Luis Sandrini, the Teatro Independencia and the Teatro La Compañía.

León Repetur

Director of Institutional Relations

Co-founder of the Cantapueblo Festival. Cultural manager, winemaker and pharmacist. He was Secretary of Culture for the Province of Mendoza.

Gustavo Aguilera

Executive Producer

Executive Producer of Cantapueblo since 2006, and General Coordinator of Cantapueblo Niños since its first edition. Senior Technician in Sociocultural Management. President and founder of the Gestores Culturales de Mendoza association.
